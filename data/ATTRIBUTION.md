# Data attribution

All four source datasets are distributed by the UCI Machine Learning Repository under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Raw archives are preserved. Processed data collapse exact full feature-target rows, retain the first original row, and select heating load for the energy task. Red and white wine remain separate tasks.

| Source | Creators and citation year | Identifier | Source page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Airfoil Self-Noise | T. Brooks, D. Pope, M. Marcolini (1989) | 10.24432/C5VW2C | https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/291/airfoil+self+noise |
| Concrete Compressive Strength | I.-C. Yeh (1998) | 10.24432/C5PK67 | https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/165/concrete+compressive+strength |
| Energy Efficiency | A. Tsanas, A. Xifara (2012) | 10.24432/C51307 | https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/242/energy+efficiency |
| Wine Quality | P. Cortez, A. Cerdeira, F. Almeida, T. Matos, J. Reis (2009) | 10.24432/C56S3T | https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/186/wine+quality |

Downloaded 27 October 2026. `download_manifest.json` identifies the exact archive URLs and hashes. The processed task counts are recorded in `results/datasets.csv`. The controlled copy allocations are generated by the experiment and do not assert that source data duplication was accidental.
